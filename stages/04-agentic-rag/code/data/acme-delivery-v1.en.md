# Acme delivery and invoice notes

Fictional teaching material; not a real merchant policy.

## delivery | Delivery times and records

Standard delivery normally takes 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. This is not a guaranteed arrival date and is not counted from order placement. Holidays, incomplete addresses, or carrier delays require checking the shipment history. Looking up a shipment does not change its status.

This note contains no per-order tracking data. It cannot establish the current location of a particular parcel; that requires an authorized order and shipping system. A delivery estimate is not a refund request window.

## invoice | Checking invoice information

Electronic invoices are issued from actual payment records. To correct an invoice title, support checks the order ID and original invoice information before referring the change to the invoicing workflow. Correcting invoice details does not change the amount paid and does not automatically initiate a refund.

This note describes a procedure, not a customer's actual invoice. Retrieving it does not justify inventing a tax number, payment amount, or invoice ID.

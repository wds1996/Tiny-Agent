# Research grounding rubric

## Pass

- every substantive claim has an existing citation;
- cited full text supports the claim at the stated strength;
- metadata is used only for bibliographic/discovery facts;
- uncertainty and evidence limits are explicit.

## Revise

- citation exists but supports only part of the claim;
- language is stronger than the evidence;
- important caveats or conflicting evidence are omitted.

## Reject / abstain

- citation is invented;
- no substantive evidence supports the requested conclusion;
- the answer infers experimental findings from a title/abstract metadata record alone.
